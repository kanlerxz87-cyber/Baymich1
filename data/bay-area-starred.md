## Irregularities and open items

1. **Count conflict in repo:** `README.md`, `data/michelin_bay_area_stars.json`, `index.html` and `scripts/build_site.py` use 41 starred restaurants (7×3★, Wine Country list, “27 one-star”). The official SF-region page shows 29 (4/7/18). The 41 count covers nine Bay Area counties, not just the SF region.
2. **Stale rows in `data/restaurants.json`:** several rows (Sons & Daughters, Lazy Bear, Quince, Californios, Saison, Atelier Crenn) carry prices/hours that do not match the official pages captured here. Not changed in this pass; recommend replacing with this file after review.
3. **Quince renovation note:** `restaurants.json` says closed Jan–Nov 2026 with a Nov 28 reopening. This is not on the official page (which shows Tue–Sat hours), so it is treated as unverified.
4. **Locale mismatch:** `michelin_bay_area_stars.json` uses `guide.michelin.com/ca/en/...`; the captured official pages use `/us/en/`. Both resolve; flag for consistency.
5. **Naides:** official page shows no phone number.
6. **Sun Moon Studio:** official page shows website but no phone.
7. **Niku Steakhouse:** hours appeared on a later page chunk only; captured in full.
8. **Outside SF region:** three official 3★ Bay Area names (Enclos, SingleThread, The French Laundry) are in the statewide article. Their restaurant pages were not captured. Remaining one- and two-star names outside the SF region are **not yet reconciled**.
