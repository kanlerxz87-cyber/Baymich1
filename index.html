06 / DECISION SUPPORT

Open questions & suggested next steps

These gaps materially affect a restaurant, hotel or itinerary choice. Each field is blank rather than inferred.

What we know / what we don't

Which restaurants currently have stars?

Known: Michelin's official California 2026 star roster, published June 24, 2026, supports the status shown for included restaurants. Unknown: any post-publication change or live operational status. Verify on Michelin before booking ↗

What is dinner for two, all-in?

Unknown: current menu, supplements, beverage choices, tax, service charge and tip policy. Need official restaurant menus/booking pages, date and a consistent alcohol assumption. Totals remain blank until all components can be sourced.

What days, hours and exceptions apply?

Unknown: most current official schedules and holiday/seasonal exceptions. Need each restaurant's official reservation page or direct operating-hours page; a third-party listing is not sufficient.

Which hotels should rank first?

Unknown: stay dates, party size, room standard, walk/transit radius and official all-in rates. Candidate hotel names have Michelin Guide citations, but nearby counts are not geocoded. See scoring plan above.

What does “Bay Area” mean for this guide?

Assumption disclosed: included star locations span San Francisco, East Bay, Peninsula, Marin and Wine Country communities named in Michelin's 2026 selection. Final boundary (e.g. include Monterey, Sacramento, or only nine Bay counties) needs a scope decision.

Which non-starred Michelin Guide restaurants are included?

Incomplete: Michelin's 2026 starred roster is complete for the included region, but the full broader Guide selection (Recommended/Bib Gourmand) has not been exhaustively exported and checked. Maria Isabel is the single recommended example included because the official 2026 article explicitly names it.

Which social videos should be linked?

Unknown: official venue-owned Instagram, TikTok and YouTube profiles / best current videos. Add only accounts linked from the venue's official website or verified brand channels.
