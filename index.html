What this site is

A clean, mobile-friendly dashboard of the MICHELIN Guide California 2025 selection for the San Francisco Bay Area. Every entry shows whether the venue is merely listed in the Guide or currently holds a Michelin star, together with verified pricing (estimate range for two adults), days and hours open, official venue link, a link to the MICHELIN Guide listing, and any YouTube / TikTok / Instagram links we could verify.

  • Data standard: Every table row links to an official source. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged unverified — nothing is inferred or invented.
  • Pricing: Shown as an estimated total for 2 adults (food only, before tax/service/drinks) where a tasting menu or menu price is published; otherwise an estimate range is given and flagged.
  • Verification status: All entries verified line by line against official venue websites or the MICHELIN Guide. Irregularities are noted on the Suggestions page.

Cluster view: where the stars are

Michelin-starred restaurants cluster in San Francisco (SoMa, Mission, Nob Hill, Jackson Square, Cow Hollow, Hayes Valley, Financial District), Napa/Sonoma wine country (Yountville, St. Helena, Healdsburg, Calistoga, Rutherford, Geyserville, Sonoma), the Peninsula (Palo Alto, Redwood City, San Mateo, Woodside, Saratoga), and the East Bay (Oakland, Berkeley, San Anselmo).
