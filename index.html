2026 star snapshot

The official 2026 California roster contains 41 starred restaurants across the cities included in this project’s Bay Area scope. The count is grouped by city cluster, not by walking distance.

◎

Star ≠ guide listing

Stars are shown separately from the 30 Bib Gourmand restaurants included here. Bib Gourmand is a Guide distinction for good quality, good-value cooking—not a Michelin star.

⌂

Stay choices

Four hotels are shortlisted from the Michelin hotel selection. Michelin Keys rate hotels; they are not restaurant stars. Exact nearby-restaurant counts and date-specific rates are not verified.

Knowns · unknowns · what must be answered

Decision brief

A clear record of what can be concluded from verified sources today—and what cannot reasonably be decided yet.

Verified enough to use

  • Current star status: the 2026 California selection was revealed June 24, 2026; the official Guide article lists the named 3-, 2- and 1-star restaurants.
  • Project coverage: 41 starred names in the declared city clusters; 25 are in San Francisco.
  • Different distinction: Bib Gourmand entries are labeled separately and never described as starred.
  • Hotels: four candidate properties and their Michelin Keys/neighborhoods are sourced from official Michelin hotel listings.

Still open — blocks ranking or budget decisions

  • What does “nearby” mean? Walking distance, transit time, drive time, or a radius? Exact hotel-to-restaurant counts depend on this.
  • When is the trip? Hours, special closures, room rates and booking inventory are date-sensitive; dates are unknown.
  • What is the meal budget? The requested two-adult total needs menu price, tax, service charge, drinks, supplements and dietary assumptions. Official all-in totals were not found.
  • Which restaurants matter? Star tier, cuisine, dietary needs, reservation lead time and accessibility are not ranked or filtered beyond current distinction/area.
  • Do social links need to be official accounts? Yes for this version; no social link is filled unless confirmed from an official restaurant source.
  • What does “Bay Area” include? This version uses the named MICHELIN roster cities shown above; it does not claim to cover every adjacent county or an arbitrary driving radius.
