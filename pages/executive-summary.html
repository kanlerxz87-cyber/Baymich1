Michelin Key hotels (Bay Area)

1. The three-star tier (worth a special journey)

  • Atelier Crenn (Cow Hollow, SF) — French/seafood, 3★ + Green Star
  • Benu (SoMa, SF) — Asian–French, 3★ — tasting $425 pp + 22% service (official)
  • Quince (Jackson Square, SF) — Californian/Italian, 3★ + Green Star — gastronomy $390 pp
  • The French Laundry (Yountville, Napa) — 3★
  • SingleThread (Healdsburg, Sonoma) — 3★ + Green Star

2. The two-star tier (worth a detour)

  • Acquerello (Nob Hill), Aubergine (Carmel), Birdsong (SoMa), Californios (SoMa), Commis (Oakland), Enclos (Sonoma, +Green), Harbor House (Elk), Kiln (Hayes Valley — $315 pp + 20%), Lazy Bear (Mission — $295 pp + 20% + $5), Saison (South Beach — from $368 pp), Sons & Daughters (Mission — $315 pp + 20%, +Green, moved Nov 2025)

3. Michelin Key hotels (where to stay)

  • 3 Keys (extraordinary): SingleThread Inn (Healdsburg, on-site 3-star restaurant), Auberge du Soleil (Rutherford, on-site 1-star), Meadowood Napa Valley (St. Helena), Canyon Ranch Woodside (closed late Oct 2025)
  • 2 Keys (exceptional): Four Seasons SF at Embarcadero, The Battery (SF), Cavallo Point Lodge (Sausalito), Bardessono & North Block (Yountville), Milliken Creek Inn (Napa), Bernardus Lodge (Carmel Valley), Montage Healdsburg, The Madrona (Healdsburg)
  • 1 Key: 1 Hotel SF, Hotel Drisco (SF), Rancho Caymus Inn (Rutherford)

4. Starred-restaurant clusters (where to base yourself)

  • San Francisco: SoMa (Benu, Birdsong, Californios, Saison, Lazy Bear, State Bird), Nob Hill (Acquerello, The Progress), Jackson Square (Quince), Cow Hollow (Atelier Crenn), Hayes Valley (Kiln), Mission (Lazy Bear, Sons & Daughters, San Ho Won), Chinatown (Mister Jiu's), FiDi (Angler)
  • Napa/Sonoma wine country: Yountville (French Laundry), St. Helena (Press), Calistoga (Auro), Rutherford (Auberge du Soleil), Geyserville (Cyrus), Healdsburg (SingleThread), Sonoma (Enclos)
  • Peninsula: Palo Alto (Protégé), Redwood City/Atherton (Selby's), Woodside (Village Pub), Saratoga (Plumed Horse), San Mateo (Wakuriya)
  • East Bay: Oakland (Commis, Sun Moon Studio), San Anselmo (Madcap)
