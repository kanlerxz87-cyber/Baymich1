Key findings

  1. San Francisco holds 25 of 41 starred restaurants (61%), including 4 of 7 three-star places (Atelier Crenn, Benu, Californios, Quince). [A]
  2. Wine Country is the second cluster: Napa (4) and Sonoma (4) together hold 8 starred restaurants, including The French Laundry, Enclos and SingleThread at three stars. [A] [B]
  3. Peninsula, South Bay, East Bay, Marin: the 5 one-star places in San Mateo and Santa Clara counties, 2 in Oakland (Commis at two stars, Sun Moon Studio at one), and 1 in San Anselmo (Madcap). County labels are general geography, see F12. [A]
  4. 2026 changes: Californios (SF) and Enclos (Sonoma) moved to three stars. New one-star entries include Naides and Wolfsbane (SF) and Troubadour (Healdsburg). [A] [B]
  5. Price (Michelin category, not a dollar amount): tiers were read for the 14 two- and three-star places (all $$$$). One-star tiers are not captured yet. The Michelin pages read so far show tiers, not dollar amounts, so couple costs are left unverified. [C] [D]
