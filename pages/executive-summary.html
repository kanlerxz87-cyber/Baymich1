Executive summary

Michelin Guide San Francisco Bay Area — 2026 edition (stars announced June 24, 2026). All distinction data verified against the official MICHELIN Guide (guide.michelin.com).

Dataset: 29 Michelin-starred (4 three-star • 7 two-star • 18 one-star) • 30 Bib Gourmand guide-listed (no star) • 86 additional selected restaurants.

Key findings

  • San Francisco dominates. 23 of 29 starred restaurants are in San Francisco, clustered in SoMa, Nob Hill, Jackson Square/Financial District, Mission, Fillmore/Japantown and Chinatown. A central SF hotel puts most starred meals within a short trip.
  • Three-star tier (4): Atelier Crenn, Benu, Californios (world’s first three-star Mexican restaurant), Quince. All $$$$.
  • Two-star tier (7): Acquerello, Birdsong, Commis (Oakland), Kiln, Lazy Bear, Saison, Sons & Daughters.
  • Oakland is the East Bay anchor. Commis (2-star) plus Sun Moon Studio (1-star) and the deepest Bib Gourmand bench (MAMA, Soba Ichi, Bombera, Jo’s Modern Thai, FOB Kitchen, Snail Bar).
  • Peninsula / Marin are single-outlier starred spots: Wakuriya (San Mateo, 1-star), Madcap (San Anselmo, 1-star). Best paired with a local hotel stay, not a day trip from SF.

Hotel guidance

For the most Michelin-starred access, stay in central San Francisco (SoMa / Union Square / Nob Hill / Financial District). Michelin-listed options include San Francisco Proper Hotel, Hotel Zeppelin, The Battery and Hotel Zetta. For wine-country starred dining, consider SingleThread Inn (Healdsburg, 3-star on property) or Auberge du Soleil (Rutherford, 1-star on property). Hotel nearby-starred counts are reasoned estimates — verify before booking.
