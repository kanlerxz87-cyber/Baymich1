7. Suggestions to discover missing information

  • Fetch each venue's official website (listed in the Source column) and record price, hours, days, exceptions, and social handles.
  • Cross-check star status against the official MICHELIN Guide (guide.michelin.com) — treat press articles as secondary, MICHELIN as authoritative.
  • For social links, search "[venue name] Instagram/TikTok/YouTube" and link only verified official accounts.
  • Set a "last verified" date per row and re-verify quarterly or before each MICHELIN annual announcement.
  • Log every irregularity (e.g., Kiln homepage still shows a one-star badge though it is two-star) in COORDINATION.md under Flagged for Review.

Bottom line: No booking or hotel decision should rest solely on this site until items 1–6 are resolved and each table row carries an official source link or is explicitly blank/unverified. This site is a planning dashboard, not a reservation system.
