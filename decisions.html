Questions that affect a reasonable decision

Are the stars current?

The roster comes from Michelin's official California 2026 star list, published June 24, 2026. It is a dated snapshot; recheck Michelin's current restaurant profile before booking. Official roster ↗

What is the all-in dinner cost for two?

For most restaurants, current published menus, supplements, drinks, taxes, service fees and tip policy are not verified. Commis has an official $279/person menu and a 20% service charge: $669.60 for two before sales tax and drinks. Remaining totals are blank.

What days/hours and exceptions apply?

Most venues' current official schedules, holiday closures and seasonal exceptions still require direct confirmation. The restaurant table leaves schedule cells blank instead of borrowing unverified hours from aggregators.

Which hotel ranks highest for Michelin access?

Not established. We need verified addresses, walking/transit distance thresholds, live official quotes for the same stay dates/room/party, and tax/fee/cancellation details.

What geographic boundary counts as the Bay Area?

This snapshot includes Michelin-listed star locations in San Francisco, Oakland, Peninsula communities, Wine Country communities and Marin. Whether Monterey, Sacramento or a narrower nine-county Bay Area should be included needs a scope decision.

Are all Michelin Guide-listed restaurants included?

No. All regional starred entries from the Michelin 2026 star roster are shown; the full set of non-starred Recommended and Bib Gourmand selections has not been exhaustively audited. Maria Isabel is included as a clearly labeled Recommended example from Michelin's article.

Which social/video links should appear?

Only accounts linked from a restaurant's own site or verified official channel are appropriate. One Atelier Crenn Instagram is directly linked by its official site; other accounts remain blank until independently verified.

Which activities fit the trip?

Trip dates, interests, budget, mobility, and transport preferences are unknown. Activity listings remain empty until operator pages establish the schedule, exceptions and reservation conditions.
